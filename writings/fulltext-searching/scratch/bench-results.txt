KEY FINDINGS:
  1. SA and FM-index are 10000x-50000x faster than ugrep for substring counting
  2. FM-index is fastest for short patterns 0.068us for 2-char th
  3. SA is more stable across pattern lengths 0.162-0.262us
  4. FM-index scales with pattern length 0.068-0.700us backward search
  5. Trigram is fast for rare patterns but slow for common ones the 0.110ms
  6. Trigram CANNOT handle patterns less than 3 chars falls back to brute force
  7. ugrep index is SLOWER than no index for single file designed for multi-file
  8. ugrep counts matching lines not total occurrences different workload
  9. FM-index build is very fast 0.02s but requires SA first 7.25s
  10. SA build is the bottleneck prefix-doubling with qsort O n log2 n
